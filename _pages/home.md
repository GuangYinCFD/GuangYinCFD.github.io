<p>I am an Associate Professor in Offshore Technology at the University of Stavanger, Norway. My research interests include fluid mechanics, turbulence modeling, fluid-structure-interaction, data-driven method and reduced-order modeling.</p>

<h2>Research interests</h2><ul>
  <li>Ocean Engineering</li>
  <li>Fluid mechanics and turbulence modeling</li>
  <li>Marine Computational Fluid Dynamics</li>
  <li>Fluid–structure interaction</li>
  <li>Data-driven methods and reduced-order modeling</li>
  <li>Hydrodynamics on Offshore and Subsea Structure</li>
</ul>
